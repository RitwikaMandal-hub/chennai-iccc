CHENNAI AI ICCC OPERATIONAL KNOWLEDGE

SECTION 1: FLOOD RESPONSE

When water-level sensors indicate a critical flood condition,
the control room should verify the reading using nearby sensors
and available rainfall information.

If multiple nearby sensors indicate rising water levels,
the incident should be treated as a potentially genuine flood
condition.

Operators should identify affected roads and vulnerable areas,
monitor traffic conditions, and coordinate emergency response.

Emergency routes should be checked before recommending
ambulance or rescue movement.

AI-generated recommendations must be reviewed by an authorised
human operator before operational actions are executed.


SECTION 2: TRAFFIC INCIDENT RESPONSE

When a major traffic incident is detected, the control room
should assess the incident location, current traffic conditions,
nearby junction congestion, and available emergency resources.

Emergency vehicle routes should be evaluated before dispatch.

Where appropriate, traffic signal priority may be considered
to facilitate emergency movement.

Any operational signal change or road-management action should
require authorised human approval.


SECTION 3: AMBULANCE RESPONSE

When an emergency medical incident is detected, the system
should identify an available nearby ambulance.

The recommended ambulance route should consider current
traffic conditions and known road restrictions.

The estimated travel time should be communicated to the
operator.

The AI system may recommend ambulance dispatch and route
selection, but final operational approval must remain with
an authorised human operator.


SECTION 4: SENSOR VALIDATION

Sensor readings should not automatically be treated as valid
when they significantly differ from nearby sensors.

The system should compare the reading with other sensors of
the same type and consider recent historical values.

If a sensor appears unreliable, the system should flag it as
potentially anomalous and use available neighbouring data
where appropriate.

Operators should be informed when sensor reliability is low.


SECTION 5: AI SAFETY

The AI Operations Agent is a decision-support system.

AI recommendations must be explainable to the operator.

Emergency actions such as ambulance dispatch, signal changes,
road closures, traffic rerouting, and emergency notifications
require human approval before execution.

The AI system must not claim that an operational action was
executed unless an execution service confirms that the action
was actually performed.


SECTION 6: DIGITAL TWIN WHAT-IF ANALYSIS

The digital twin may be used to simulate potential situations
before operational decisions are made.

Example scenarios include increased traffic, extreme rainfall,
road closure, emergency incidents, and sensor failure.

Scenario results should be clearly identified as simulations
and should not be presented as actual observations.

Operators can compare predicted outcomes and use the results
as decision-support information.
